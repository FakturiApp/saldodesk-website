The rights of each account are changed in **Settings** → **Accounts**. On the screen the rights are called "permissions". To open the account you need the **Manage accounts** permission. To change the role or a permission, you also need the **Manage permissions** permission.

1. At the top right, click your name, then **Settings**.
2. Click **Accounts**.
3. In the account's row, click **Edit**. Its editing panel opens above the list.

## New role
This needs the **Manage permissions** permission. Without it the **Role** field is gray and there is no **Save** below it.
1. In **Role**, choose the new role.
2. Click **Save** below it. It says "The role is saved".

The account then has exactly the role's permissions. Permissions that were ticked separately before are removed.

## One permission more or less
This too needs the **Manage permissions** permission. Without it the **Show every permission** button isn't there.
1. Click **Show every permission**.
2. Tick or untick the permission.

3. Click **Save** below the permissions.

Such an account has "its own permissions", and that is written next to **Show every permission**. With **Back to the role**, it again gets only the role's permissions.

## Other things in editing
- **Full name**: change it and click **Save**. The new name appears as "Prepared by" on all documents of that account, the old ones too. So only correct the name, for example a typo.
- **Change username**: enter the **New username**, in **Confirm the current username** write the old one, and in **Your current password** your own password. The person then signs in with the new name.
- **Switch mode**: what **Switch user** asks for on this account — see [How do I switch to another user on the same computer?](../smeni-korisnik/).

When you're done, click **Close** in the row.

## Turn off an account
When someone stops working, click **Deactivate** in their row. They are signed out right away and can no longer sign in. The row turns pale.

An account is not deleted. The name stays on all documents they made. With **Activate**, the account works again.

For a new employee, always open a new account — see [How do I open an account for a new employee?](../nova-smetka/). If you change the name on the account of someone who left, their old documents will carry the new person's name.

> **Important**
> You can't change the permissions of an account that has more permissions than you, or give a permission you don't have yourself. You can't deactivate yourself. The last active account with the **Manage accounts** permission can't be deactivated or lose that permission — the company must always have someone who manages the accounts.
