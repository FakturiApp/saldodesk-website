Every change to a product is recorded: who made it, when, and what it said before and after.

1. In **Products**, click the product.
2. Click the **Changes** tab.

<figure class="shot"><span class="shot-frame" style="aspect-ratio:1280/720"><img src="/help-img/en/products-promeni.webp" alt="The product's Changes tab" width="1280" height="720" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><span class="shot-ring" style="left:37.30%;top:32.68%;width:6.42%;height:4.72%"></span><span class="shot-tag is-above is-right" style="bottom:67.32%;left:40.51%">Click here</span></span><figcaption>The product's Changes tab</figcaption></figure>
3. The newest change is at the top. Each one shows what was done (for example **Edited** or **Change prices**), who did it and when. Below that is every field that changed: the old value → the new one.
4. If there are many changes, click **Load more** at the bottom to see the older ones.

Here you also see changes made to many products at once — a new price, category, minimum stock — because each one is recorded on every product separately.

Stock coming in and going out is not shown here. That is on the **Stock card** tab — see [How much is in stock, and in which warehouse?](../zaliha-na-proizvod/).

Changes cannot be deleted or edited. If something was changed by mistake, put the old value back on the **Basic** tab and click **Save** — that will be recorded too.
