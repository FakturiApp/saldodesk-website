The partner card is everything about a partner in one place. You open it by clicking the partner in **Partners**.

## At the top, below the name
Below the name are the phone (click to call), the contact people, how much the partner still owes (**Debt**) and their **Payment deadline (days)**. So when a customer calls, you don't have to look it up.

Next to the name are tags: **Customer**, **Supplier**, **Individual**, **Foreign**, **Inactive** and **e-invoice ready ✓** or **e-invoice ✗**.

The debt and the deadline are visible only to an account with the **View payments & debt** permission. A salesperson without that permission doesn't see them. If the customer has a credit limit, instead of them the salesperson sees whether the customer may still buy on credit — see [How do I set a credit limit for a customer?](../partner-kreditni-limit/).

- **+ New document** opens a new document with this partner already chosen.
- **Compare** puts the partner in a comparison with others — see [How do I compare partners, products or documents?](../sporedi/).

## The tabs
- **Basic** — the partner's details. Change what you need and click **Save**.
- **Discounts** — the agreed discounts. See [How do I give a customer an agreed discount?](../partner-popust/).
- **Open items** and **Account card** — what they owe, what they paid and the balance. See [How much does a partner owe me?](../partner-dolg/).
- **Documents** — all documents with the partner. At the top it shows **Turnover**, **Overdue invoices** and **Debt**. The documents are split into **Sales**, **Purchasing** and the other groups, as in **Documents**.
- **Products** — every product we have sold them, the most recently sold first. For each one it shows the **Last price** (with the discount), the last quantity, the date and the document. So you quickly see whether, and at what price, we sold them something.
- **Service** — the client's service orders and devices. It is there only if the company has Service.
- **Rentals** — the client's rental machines and contracts. It is there only if the company has Rentals.
- **History** — every change to the partner: what was changed, when and who changed it.

## In Products
In **Columns & filters** you can turn on more columns, among them **Bought in total** and **Documents**. These two count only invoices and printed fiscal receipts, minus the return notes. A dispatch note is counted once it is invoiced.
