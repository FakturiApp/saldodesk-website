For example: all toners from one brand go up 5 %. You don't have to change them one by one.

1. At the top, click **Products**.
2. Find the products. Type part of the name in the search field above the table, or in the table header click the filter sign under **Category** (or **Brand**) and choose.
3. Tick the products (the box left of the name). The box at the top, in the table header, ticks them all.
4. A bar with the number selected appears above the table. Click **Change prices**.
5. Under **Which price**, choose **Selling price** or **Dealer price**.
6. Under **How**, choose:
   - **By a percentage** — for example 5 for a 5 % increase, or -5 for a decrease;
   - **By an amount** — how many denars to add (or take away, with a minus);
   - **A new price** — the same price for all selected;
   - **With VAT to whole denars** — the price with VAT of each product is rounded to a whole denar. You don't enter a number here. This is how you fix prices saved earlier that read, for example, 100,01 instead of 100. The list also shows the price with VAT before and after the change;
   - **Markup on the cost** — a percentage above the cost price that **Products** shows;
   - **Kept markup** — each product by its own markup, or by its category's markup. You don't enter a number here.

   The last two are there only for an account with the permission to see cost prices. There the price with VAT is rounded to whole denars.
7. Enter the number and click **Show**. A list appears with the old and new price of each product.

8. If it's all right, click **Save**. The button says how many products will change.

The prices are without VAT. With **By a percentage** and **By an amount**, the new price is rounded to two decimals. A price made from a price with VAT (**With VAT to whole denars** and both markups) is kept with four decimals, so the price with VAT stays whole. With **By a percentage** and **By an amount**, a product that doesn't have that price isn't changed — the list says "no price — not changed". With **A new price**, such a product gets the new price too.

Every change is recorded on the product itself, in the **Changes** tab.

If someone changed the price of one of the selected products in the meantime, nothing is saved and the app tells you which ones. Click **Show** again.

You can come here from a purchase with the products already ticked — see [How do I change selling prices when a new delivery arrives?](../novi-ceni-od-nabavka/).

Nothing changes until you click **Save**. If the number is wrong, click **Cancel** — the prices stay the same.
