All three change stock without a sale: they have no customer and you do not enter a price. You find them in **Documents** → **Internal**.

- **Write-off** — the goods go out but were not sold: used up in the company, damaged, past their expiry date, given as a sample.
- **Adjustment** — there is more or less on the shelf than the app says, so you correct the figure.
- **Disposal** — the goods were handed to a scrap buyer, for example an old machine. You need the document the scrap buyer gave you.

## Write-off
1. In **Internal**, click **Write-off**, then the blue "+" button.
2. If the company has more than one warehouse, choose the **Warehouse**.
3. Write the **Reason**. It is required.
4. Enter the products and, in **Qty**, how many are going out. If the product has serial numbers, choose which pieces.
5. Click **Issue**.

The app takes the cost value by itself.

## Adjustment
1. In **Internal**, click **Adjustment**, then the blue "+" button.
2. If the company has more than one warehouse, choose the **Warehouse**.
3. Write the **Reason**. It is required.
4. For each product, in **Corrected qty**, enter how many there really are on the shelf — not the difference.
5. Click **Issue**.

On the issued adjustment, below the quantity it says for example "difference +22" or "difference −3": that is how much stock changed. If the quantity goes up, the added pieces are valued at the average cost price.
## Disposal
1. In **Internal**, click **Disposal**, then the blue "+" button.
2. If the company has more than one warehouse, choose the **Warehouse**.
3. In **Scrap recipient**, choose the scrap buyer. If they are not there, click "+ New partner", enter a name and phone and click **Save**. (This button is there only if your account may add partners.)
4. From their document, copy the **Recipient's document — number** and **Recipient's document — date**. The date cannot be after today.
5. Enter the products and the **Qty**. If the product has serial numbers, choose each piece that is leaving.
6. Click **Issue**. If you do not have the recipient's document yet, click **Save draft** and issue it later.

On the open disposal, in the **Scan of the document** section, you can add a picture or PDF of the recipient's document.

> **Important**
> A disposal cannot be issued without the recipient's document: recipient, number and date. On an issued disposal they can be corrected, but not deleted.

## If you made a mistake
All three have **Edit** and **Void**. **Void** puts stock back the way it was before the document.
