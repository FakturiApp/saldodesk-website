At the top left, next to the SaldoDesk logo, is the **Search** field. From it you search for partners, products, documents and everything else, from any page.

1. Click in the **Search** field (or press Ctrl and K).
2. Type part of the name, a number, code, phone or city. For example "bitola" or "invoice 7".
3. The results appear right away, in groups: **Partners**, **Products**, **Documents** and the others.
4. Click a result to open it.

Below each result it says where it was found, for example "Found in: City".

## You don't have to type it exactly
- You can type in Latin letters: "kopir centar" finds "Kopir Centar" written in Cyrillic.
- A small typo doesn't matter. If there is no exact match, at the top it says "No exact match for …" and the results for the closest word are shown.
- Several words are searched together: "komerc bitola" finds the partner with both words.

## Full search
If there are many results, press Enter or click **Show all** next to the group. The **Search** page opens. There:
- at the top, choose a group, for example **Partners** or **Documents**;
- below it there are filters for that group — for partners, for example, **Customer**, **Supplier**, **Individual**, **Include inactive** and **City**.

## Recent searches
When you click in the empty field, **Recently opened** records and **Recent searches** are shown. This is remembered only on your computer. If you don't want it shown: **My profile** → **Windows and cards** → **History in the search box** → **Hide**.

## Scanning
The camera button, next to the field, is **Scan with the camera**. Point the phone's or computer's camera at a QR code or barcode — the code is entered into the search by itself.

If the code belongs to a record (document, product, machine…), the app goes straight to it. For a box with a quantity or for a serial number, a card is shown first. If the code isn't known, **Search** opens with the code. If you want a small card with the record to show first: **My profile** → **Windows and cards** → **A scan (camera or scanner)** → **A card first**.

## Searching in one list
Every list (**Partners**, **Products**, **Documents**) also has its own search field above the table. It searches only in that list.
