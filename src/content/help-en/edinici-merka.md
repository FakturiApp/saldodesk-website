Every product has one **Unit of measure**: pcs, kg, l, m… It is printed on documents next to the quantity.

## New unit
The quickest way is from the product itself:
1. Open the product (or **+ New product**).
2. Click in the **Unit of measure** field and type the new unit.
3. Click "＋ Add" at the bottom of the list.
4. Click **Save** on the product.

## All units in one place
1. On the product form, under **Unit of measure**, click **Manage units**.
2. The **Units of measure** window opens. Each unit has a name in Macedonian (MK), English (EN) and Albanian (SQ).

3. To rename one, change the name in the field and click **Save** in that row.
4. For a new unit, type it at the bottom in "New unit (code)…" and click **Add**.
5. Delete a unit you don't need with **Delete**.

The name is printed on documents in the document's language, and the Macedonian one also goes into the e-Invoice to UJP (the Public Revenue Office). The name can have at most 10 characters.

A unit that at least one product uses cannot be deleted. Neither can **pcs**, because every new product gets it.

> **Important**
> A product has only one unit — the app does not convert boxes into pieces. If you buy in boxes and sell by the piece, keep the product by the piece and enter on the goods receipt how many pieces arrived.
