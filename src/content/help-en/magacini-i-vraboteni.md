Turnover by warehouse and by employee is in **Reports** → **Warehouses & employees**.

1. At the top, click **Reports**, then **Warehouses & employees**.
2. Pick a period, just as in **By period**: **This month**, **Last month**… or **Custom period**.
3. Click **Warehouse** for turnover by warehouse, or **Employee** for turnover by employee.
4. Pick the order: **By turnover**, **By documents**, **By profit** or **Lowest margin**.

5. For **Warehouse**, a click on a row opens **By period** for that warehouse only.
6. **Export to Excel** takes the same list.

## Who the "employee" is
The employee is whoever prepared the document. Documents are counted, not work. If someone made the invoice instead of a colleague, the turnover goes to the one who made it.

Documents without a preparer, for example those the app made by itself, are in the **Automatic** row.

## Warehouse
A storno and a credit note count in the warehouse of the invoice they correct. A document without a warehouse is in the **No warehouse** row.

Only an account with the **Costs & margins** right sees **Profit** and **Lowest margin**.
