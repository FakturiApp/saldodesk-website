Categories and brands are lists you make once and then just pick on the products. That way there is no "Toner" and "Toners" as two different categories.

## A new category or brand
1. At the top, click **Products**.
2. Click **Categories and brands**. At the top of the window, pick **Categories** or **Brands**.
3. At the very bottom, click **New category** (or **New brand**), type a name and click **Add**.
In the same window:
- **sub-level** next to a category makes a subcategory in it, for example Toners → HP. The levels can go as deep as you like.
- Click the name — to rename it and enter **Warranty (months)**, then **Save**. Products in that category or brand get that warranty if they have none of their own.
- For a category, the same row also has **Selling markup %** and **Dealer markup %**. Products and subcategories that have none of their own get it. The new prices are suggested from it when a new purchase arrives. Only accounts with the **Costs & margins** right see it.
- ▲ and ▼ change the order.
- 🗑 removes it. It is deleted only if no product uses it, there is nothing under it and no other part of the app uses it (for example a packaging type in Waste). Otherwise it is only hidden from the choices, and the products keep it.

You can also add a new category or brand from the product itself: click in the **Category** or **Brand** field, type a new name and click "＋ Add" at the bottom of the list.

## Several products into the same category at once
1. In **Products**, tick the products.
2. In the bar above the table, click **Assign…**.
3. Pick a **Category** and/or **Brand**. If you like, type tags in **Add tags (comma-separated)**.
4. Click **Apply**.

Whatever is left at "— don't change —" stays as it is. **No category** (or **No brand**) takes the category (or brand) off the selected products.

> **Important**
> Renaming a category takes effect everywhere at once — on all the products that have it. Do not make a new category just to change the name.
