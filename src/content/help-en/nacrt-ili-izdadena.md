An invoice is either a draft or issued. Which one it is, it says at the top, next to its name: **Draft** or **Issued**.

## Draft
A draft is an invoice you are still writing.

- It has no number.
- The goods are still in stock, and the customer owes nothing.
- It can be changed with **Edit** and deleted with **Delete draft**.
- If you print it, DRAFT is written across the sheet.
- A draft nobody has touched for 60 days is usually deleted by the app itself. It is kept if another document or order points to it.

## Issued invoice
An issued invoice is a real invoice.

- It gets a number.
- The goods leave stock, and the customer owes the amount.
- Until it is sent to UJP (the Public Revenue Office), it can usually still be changed with **Edit**. Exceptions: an invoice to a private person without a tax number or to a foreign customer, once it has been printed or once you have clicked **Handed to the buyer** (see [How do I print the invoice?](../pechati-faktura/)), and an invoice dated within a period for which VAT has already been filed (see [Why can't I change a document from a filed VAT period?](../podnesen-ddv-period/)).
- If it is wrong, cancel it — see [How do I cancel an issued invoice?](../ponishti-faktura/).

## Which button does what
1. On **New document**, the **Save draft** button keeps the invoice as a draft.

2. The **Issue** button issues it right away.
3. You find the draft later in **Documents**: instead of a number it has a dash "—", and the row is grayer. Open it and click **Issue**.
> **Important**
> A draft is not an invoice. Until you click **Issue**, the customer has no invoice, and the goods are still in stock.
