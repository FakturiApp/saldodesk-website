When the company starts working, the **Getting started** list sits on **Home**, above the tiles. It shows what still needs to be done, and each step opens the screen where it is done. The list is visible only to someone with the **Settings** permission.

## The steps
- **Company details** — tax number, street, number, postal code and city. Without them, an e-Invoice can't be sent. This is done in **Settings** → **Company**.
- **Warehouse** — only if you have the Warehouse operations module. Give the main warehouse a code or another name, or add a second one. This is done in **Settings** → **Warehouses**.
- **Users** — an account for everyone who will work in the program. This is done in **Settings** → **Accounts**.
- **Partners & products** — at least one partner and one product. While one part is missing, the step says which. This is done in **Settings** → **Import data**, where the opening balance of the stock is too, or by hand in **Partners** and **Products**.
- **First document** — the first issued invoice. A draft doesn't count, nor does the opening balance of the stock.

Click **Open** next to the step to go where it is done.

## They tick themselves
You don't need to tick anything. When the company does the step, it gets a ✓ by itself — when you open Home, or within 5 minutes at the latest. At the top it says how many steps are done, for example "2 of 5".

## Skip and Restore
If you don't need a step — for example you work alone and there will be no other users — click **Skip** next to it. The step is skipped for the whole company. If you change your mind, click **Restore**.

## Hide the list
Click ✕ at the top right of the list. The list is hidden only for you. A small circle with the number of steps left then sits above the "?" button in the bottom right corner. Click the circle and Home opens with the list again.

If you moved the "?" button to the top, the circle is at the top, in front of it.

## When everything is done
When every step is done or skipped, the list says "**Everything is ready.**" Click **Close**. The list and the circle disappear for everyone in the company and don't appear again.

If you don't see all the steps — for example you don't have the **Accounts** permission — the list may say "The other steps are done by another administrator." Then **Close** isn't there: the list is closed by whoever sees all the steps, once they are all done.

## During setup
When the program is set up for the first time, the third step comes after the recovery code — **Company details**: street, number, postal code, city, and the tax number if you didn't enter it in the first step. Click **Save and continue**, or **Skip** if you want to enter them later — the **Company details** step on Home will remind you.
