The travel order is the official form "Travel order for the transport of goods". The app draws the whole form on a plain A4 sheet, in landscape.

1. At the top, click **Documents**, then **Transport**.
2. Click **+ New travel order**.
3. The company, head office, place, today's date and "own needs" are already filled in. Any field can be changed.
4. In **Driver**, choose a driver from the list or type another name.
5. For the first **Vehicle**, choose a vehicle from the list: the type, make, registration number and load capacity fill in. Any field can be overwritten. The second vehicle (for example a trailer) is optional.
6. In **Route**, write the route, for example "Skopje Shtip".
7. Click **Save**.

The order gets a number based on the day in **Date of issue**: 01.11.2026 is "0111/26", the second one on the same day is "0111-2/26".

## Printing
1. On the saved order, click **Print**. A new tab opens with the form.
2. Print double-sided, flipping on the short edge, or on two sheets. Leave the margins in the print window as they are.

The first page is the summary and the front side, the second is the table of trips. The kilometers, fuel and trips are written by hand along the way.

## Afterwards
- A saved order can be changed at any time: change it and click **Save changes**. Every change stays in **History**. If you change the **Date of issue**, the order gets a new number for the new day, and the old number is not given out again.
- If you don't need the order, click **Delete**. It goes to **Deleted** (the button above the list). From there it can be restored for 30 days: open it and click **Restore** — it comes back with the same number and history. A deleted order can't be changed until you restore it. After 30 days it is deleted for good. The number is not given out again.

If saving is turned off in **Settings** → **Transport**, the order is only printed: you write the number yourself in **No.**, then click **Print**.
