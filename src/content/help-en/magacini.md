A warehouse is any place where you keep goods: the main warehouse, a shop, a service workshop, a vehicle. Stock is kept separately for each warehouse.

1. At the top, click **Products**.
2. Click **Warehouses**.
3. At the very bottom, in "New warehouse…", type the name.
4. If you like, type a **Code** in the field next to it — 1 to 5 letters or digits, for example M1.
5. Click **Add**.

The same list is also in the settings: at the top right, click your name, then **Settings**, then **Warehouses**. It works there just as in the window.

In the same window:
- To rename a warehouse, change the name in the field and click **Save** next to it.
- The **Default** warehouse is the one suggested on new documents. To make another warehouse the default, click **Make default** next to it.
- For a warehouse you no longer use, click **Archive**. **Restore** brings it back.

The code shows in the document number (for example M1-15/2026) only if, in **Settings** → **Company**, under **How the number looks**, the **Warehouse code** part has been added with **Add a part** — see [How do I change how document numbers look (prefix, year)?](../broevi-na-dokumenti/).

For how much is in each warehouse — see [How much is in stock, and in which warehouse?](../zaliha-na-proizvod/). On documents, in the **Warehouse** field, you pick which warehouse the goods leave from or go into.

> **Important**
> Goods are moved from one warehouse to another with a transfer document, not by changing the stock — see [How do I move goods from one warehouse to another?](../prenos-megju-magacini/). If one warehouse needs goods from another, it can also request them — see [How do I request goods from another warehouse?](../barania-od-magacin/).
