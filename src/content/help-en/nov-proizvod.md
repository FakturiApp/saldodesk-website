Products are in **Products**. Every product must have a **Name** and a **Code**. Everything else can be filled in later.

1. At the top, click **Products**.
2. Click **+ New product**.

3. In **Name**, type the product's name.
4. Click in **Code**. If you press the → arrow key on the keyboard, the app fills in the next free code. You can also type your own.
5. If the product has a barcode, type it or scan it into **Barcode**. If it has none and you want one, click **Generate**.
6. If you use them, pick a **Category** and **Brand**.
7. Under **Type**, leave **Product** for goods kept in stock. Pick **Service** for labor, transport and the like — a service never goes into stock.
8. The **Unit of measure** is **pcs**. Change it if the product is measured differently, for example in kg or m.
9. Enter the **Selling price** (without VAT). If you know the price with VAT, enter it in **Price incl. VAT** below it — the other field is calculated by itself.
10. Check the **VAT**.
11. Click **Save**.

The new product's card opens. It can now be used on documents.

You do not enter the cost here. It is calculated by itself from the goods receipts — see [Selling, dealer and cost price — which is which?](../ceni-na-proizvod/).

## Further down the form
Below the prices there are sections that open with a click: **Other** (weight, warranty, extra barcodes, a note; tags and the picture are there only with the "Warehouse management" module), **Declaration** (for the declaration label) and, if it is there, **Purchasing** (minimum stock and default supplier). Fill them in only if you need them.

## The same product in several variants
Open an existing product and click **Duplicate** at the top. A new product opens with the same details. The **Code** and barcodes are empty, because they must be new. Change what you need and click **Save**.

## A new product while you write a goods receipt
If the product is not there on a goods receipt, type the name in the product field and click **Create new** under it. A small window opens with **Name**, **Code** and **VAT**. Click **Create** — the product goes onto the line right away. Fill it in later (price, category…) in **Products**.

> **Important**
> Two products cannot have the same code or the same barcode. If under **Code** or **Barcode** it says "A product with … already exists", that product has already been entered. Click its name next to the message and use it instead of making a new one.
