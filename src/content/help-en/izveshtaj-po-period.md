Turnover and profit for any period are in **Reports** → **By period**. It is the same turnover you see on **Home** and in **Money**.

1. At the top, click **Reports**. **By period** opens.
2. Pick a period: **This month**, **Last month**, **This year** or **Last year**.
3. For another period, click **Custom period**, enter **From** and **To** and click **Show**.

4. If you want only a part, pick in the fields below the period: **Customer**, **Product**, **Category** or **Brand**. Three fields appear only when there is something to pick: **Partner category** if the company keeps partner categories, **Warehouse** if there are two or more warehouses, and **Employee** (who prepared the document) if two or more people prepared documents. **Clear filters** brings them all back.
5. At the top are the totals: **Turnover** (without and with VAT), **Profit**, **Margin** and **Documents**. Under each number it says how much it was in the previous period and the change in percent. For **This month**, that is last month up to the same day.
6. Above the table, pick how to split it: **By day**, **By month** or **By year**. By day works for one year at most.
7. Click a name in the **Top customer** or **Best-selling product** column. The full list of customers or products for that day, month or year opens.
8. **Export to Excel** takes the same table, with the same filters.

## The same period last year
Tick **Compare with last year**. Next to each row, **Last year** and **Change** appear — the same day, month or year from last year.

## What counts as turnover
- Invoices, export invoices and printed fiscal receipts.
- A storno is subtracted. A credit note is subtracted, a debit note is added.
- A quote, proforma and dispatch note are not turnover. A dispatch note counts when an invoice is made from it.
- Drafts and cancelled documents do not count.
- A document counts on the date written on it, not on the day it was issued.
- A document in euros or another currency counts in denars, at the rate on the document itself.

## Cost and profit
Cost is at the real price. If the supplier's price arrives later, the profit changes on the sales themselves, in their month — see [What does "unknown" instead of a cost mean?](../nabavna-nepoznata/). Services have no cost.

Only an account with the **Costs & margins** right sees the **Cost**, **Profit** and **Margin** columns. If you do not see them, ask the administrator for the right. The whole **Reports** screen needs the **View reports** right.
