The contract is valid until its end. In **Rentals** → **Contracts**, the **Validity** column says "Active", "Expiring" (less than 6 months left until the end), "Expired" or "Archived".

A contract with **Auto-renew** (the ♻ sign) extends itself by 12 months at each end and does not expire.

## Renew
1. Open the contract and click **Contract terms**.
2. In **Renew contract**, under **Term**, pick how many months, or **By original term** — as long as the **Contract term (months)**.
3. Check the date under **New end**.
4. Click **Renew**.

All the machines still on the contract get the same new end. Returned machines keep their own.

A contract with **Auto-renew** or with no end has nothing to renew by hand.

## Change the contract
In **Contract terms**, in the **Edit** section, you change the **End**, **Auto-renew**, **Contract term (months)** and **Note**. Click **Save**. The changes apply to the machines still on the contract. The **Currency** cannot be changed once a month has been calculated.

## End of the rental
- When the machine comes back, click **Return** next to it — see [The customer is returning the machine — what do I do?](../iznajmuvanje-vrati-mashina/). The contract ends with the last machine returned.
- After the contract's end, the months are no longer invoiced ("the contract has expired"), but the machine stays in **Machines** until you return it.

> **Important**
> **Archive** only hides the contract: nothing goes back into stock and nothing is charged, and the machines stay in **Machines**, with no contract. If the machine is coming back to you, use **Return**. An archived contract is brought back with **Restore from archive**.
