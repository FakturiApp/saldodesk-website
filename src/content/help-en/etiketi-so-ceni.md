A label usually has the name, the price with VAT, the code and a barcode that can be scanned. It can be printed on a label printer or on an A4 sheet of stickers.

## For several products
1. At the top, click **Products**.
2. Tick the products (the box to the left of the name).
3. In the bar above the table, click **Print labels**.
4. The labels page opens. Under **Label**, leave **Price**.
5. Under **Paper**, pick **Label printer (one per page)** or **A4 sheet of stickers**.
6. Check the **Size** in millimeters (or click one of the ready-made ones, for example 60×40) and the number of **Copies**.
7. Click **Print**.

8. The browser's print window opens. Pick the printer and print.

## For one product
Open the product and at the top right click **Price label**. From there it is the same, from step 5.

## For goods that just arrived
Open the goods receipt and at the top click **Print**, then **Price labels**. The labels page opens, with one label for each piece that arrived. Check them and click **Print**.

## Sale
For goods on discount, enter the discount in **Sale (discount %)**. The regular price is printed crossed out, and the new one large. This applies only to that printing; the product's price does not change.

In the same **Label** field you can also pick **Declaration** or **Box QR**.

> **Important**
> The label prints the price that is on the product at that moment. If you changed the price, print the labels again and stick on the new ones.
