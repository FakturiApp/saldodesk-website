Every machine a client has brought in stays on record after the repair too. When it comes back, first look at what has been done so far.

## The Machines list
1. At the top, click **Service**, then **Machines**.
2. Search by serial number, our number, type, brand or model. In **All clients**, choose a client to see only their machines.
3. Click a machine to open it.

The list also shows **Last visit**, **Open ticket**, **Visits** and **Last problem**. A new machine is not entered here, but at intake (see [How do I take a device in for service?](../servis-priem/)).

## The machine's card
At the top you see the type, brand, model, serial number and client. Under **Service visits** is every ticket for the machine, newest first, with the reported problem, the diagnosis and the work done. Click the ticket number to open it.

- **Edit**: changes the type, brand, model, serial number, our number, the default accessories and the note, and **Client** — who has the machine.
- A machine moves to another client only when it has no open ticket. The old tickets stay with the previous client. Every change shows on the card, under **Change of client**: "Moved from … to …", with the date.
- **Archive machine**: the machine is no longer offered at intake and goes to **Archived**. **Restore from archive** brings it back.
- **Sold by us**: shows if the machine was linked to one of your sales at intake.
- **Parts for this machine**: click **Choose the model** and choose the model. From then on, the parts for that model in **Products** open from here. This section shows only with the "Warehouse management" module — see [How do I mark which machines a toner or part is for?](../kompatibilnost-modeli/).

## QR sticker
1. On the card, click **Sticker**.
2. Choose **Paper**, **Size** and **Copies**, then click **Print**.

The sticker has the QR code, the serial number, the brand and model, and your company. A QR code scanned in the app opens the machine.

For several machines at once: in the **Machines** list, tick them and click **Print stickers**.
