**Home** is the first page after you sign in. It has tiles: turnover today and this month, receivables, what is late, the latest documents and more. Everyone arranges their own Home — that doesn't change Home for the others.

Everyone sees only the tiles they have permission for. If on the default Home a whole column is made of tiles you can't see, that column is not there, and the other columns are wider.

## Add a tile
1. At the top, click **Home**.
2. Click **+ Add**.
3. In the **Add a tile** window, click **Add** next to the tile you need. Where it says "already on Home", you already have that tile.

## Move a tile or change its size
- To move a tile, grab it with the mouse and drag it to a new place. The other tiles move by themselves.
- For a bigger or smaller tile, drag its bottom right corner.
- The padlock button (**Lock the page**) locks Home: nothing moves, and **+ Add** doesn't work. One more click unlocks it.

## Set up a tile
1. Hover the mouse over the tile. ⚙ appears at its top right. Click it.
2. If you want, change the **Title**, **Color** or **Icon**. Some tiles also have **Options**, for example what to compare the turnover with.
3. A tile that shows a number also has **Colour by value**: with **+ Rule**, the tile colors itself when the number is below or above the value you enter.
4. Click **Done**.

**Remove** takes the tile off Home. You can bring it back with **+ Add**.

If you want ⚙ to be visible all the time, not only when you hover over the tile: **My profile** → **Windows and cards** → **⚙ on the home tiles** → **Always visible**.

## The numbers
The numbers refresh by themselves every five minutes. To refresh right away, click the button with the circular arrow (**Refresh**).

## Start over
In the ⋯ menu (**More**), at the top right:
- **Restore the default** — Home goes back to how it was at the start. Your changes are lost.
- **Save for the company** — everyone who doesn't have their own layout sees yours, and new employees start from it. Only an account with the **Settings** permission has this.
- **Use the company’s** — instead of yours, you get the layout saved for the company. It is there only when such a layout exists.
