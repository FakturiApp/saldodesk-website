In **Settings** → **Fields** you decide which fields you see for partners and products, what they are called and where they sit on the form. **Partners** are on the left, **Products** on the right.

1. At the top right, click your name, then **Settings**.
2. Click **Fields**.
3. In **Form fields**, each field is one row:

- **Your name for it** — write the name the way you call the field. The new name applies on the form, in the lists and on printed documents.
- **Visible** — untick so the field isn't shown anywhere.
- **Printed** — untick so the field isn't printed on the documents.
- **Part** — which part of the form the field is in. **Top** is the part that is always open.
- ▲▼ — move the field up or down.

Every change is saved by itself, right away. The name is saved when you click outside the field.

For a field with 🔒, the gray boxes can't be changed: without the field the record isn't saved, UJP (the Public Revenue Office) requires it for an e-Invoice, or the law requires it to be printed. The name can always be changed.

Some fields (for example Tags and Picture for products) need the Warehouse operations module. Without it they are hidden and have 🔒; what was entered in them stays and shows again when the module is turned on.

## A new part of the form
Click **+ New part** and give it a name. Then move fields into it in **Part**. An empty part isn't shown on the form. **Restore default** brings the parts and the order back to how they were at the start.

## Your own field
If you need a field that isn't there, for example "Region", add it in **Custom fields**, below the list:
1. In **Field name**, write the name.
2. In **Type**, choose **Text**, **Number**, **Yes / No**, **List**, **Date** or **Multi-select**.
3. For a list, write the choices in **Values (comma separated)**, for example Skopje, Bitola, Ohrid.
4. Tick **Required** if the record must not be saved without it.
5. Click **Add field**.

The new field appears on the form in the **Custom fields** section. For a field you no longer need, click **Archive**.
