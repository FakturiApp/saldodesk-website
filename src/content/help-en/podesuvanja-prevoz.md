You enter the vehicles and drivers once, in **Settings** → **Transport**. After that, on the travel order and the waybill you just choose them.

## Vehicles
1. At the top right, click your name, then **Settings**.
2. Click **Transport**.
3. Under **Vehicles**, enter **Type** (for example "passenger", "truck" or "trailer" — another word works too), **Make**, **Registration number** and, if you want, **Load capacity (tonnes)**.
4. Click **Add vehicle**.

The same registration number can't be entered twice — not with different spaces, not in lowercase, not in Latin letters. "SK 2782 AU" and "sk2782au" are the same vehicle.

To change one, click **Edit** next to the vehicle, make the change and click **Save vehicle**. **Remove** takes the vehicle off the list. Orders and waybills already printed don't change.

## Drivers
1. Under **Drivers**, enter **Full name**.
2. Click **Add driver**.

## Saving
- **Save travel orders** is on. Each travel order gets a number and stays in the list under **Documents** → **Transport**.
- **Save waybills** is off. The waybill is only printed. Turn it on if you want waybills to have a number and a list too.

The text in **Additional instructions on a blank waybill** goes into field 9 of every blank waybill. Click **Save the text**.

> **Important**
> This tab is visible only to an account with the **Settings** permission. Technicians choose the vehicles and drivers on the form, but don't change them.
