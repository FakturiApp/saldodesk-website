1. Open the invoice: in **Documents**, click it.
2. Click **Print**.
3. The invoice opens just as it will come out on paper. If you need more than one copy, change the number in **Copies**. For a customer who reads English, tick **In English**.
4. Click **Print** at the top right.

5. The browser's print window opens. Choose a printer and print. There you can also save the invoice as a PDF.

A draft prints without a number, with DRAFT written across the sheet.

If the print window opens right after you click **Print**, without the page with the invoice, that is your choice in **My profile** → **Printing** (at the top right, click your name, then **My profile**). Under **The “Print” button**, choose **Preview first**. The look of the invoice is changed in **Settings** → **Printing** — see [How do I change the look of printed documents?](../izgled-pechateni/).

> **Important**
> An invoice to an individual without a tax number or to a foreign customer is locked when it is printed for the first time (or when you click **Handed to the buyer**). The app asks you before that. Click **Continue — lock and print** only if the invoice is correct. A locked invoice no longer has **Edit** or **Cancel**: it can only be corrected with a storno, a credit note or a return note — see [Storno or credit note — which one when?](../storno-ili-ko/).
