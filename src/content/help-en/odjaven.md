A sign-in does not last forever. The app takes you back to the sign-in screen in these cases:

- It says "Your session has expired. Please sign in again." — the time for which a sign-in is valid has passed. Sign in again; nothing saved is lost.
- Your account's password was changed or reset, on another computer or by the administrator. Then every place where you were signed in is signed out. You change your own password in **My profile** — see [How do I change my password, PIN, theme or language?](../moj-profil/).
- The administrator deactivated your account. Then the sign-in says "Wrong username or password." even with the right password. Ask them.
- Someone clicked **Sign out** in the menu under your name.

The locked screen ("Locked") is not a sign-out: the app locked itself because nobody was working. Click your name and carry on — see [How do I switch to another user on the same computer?](../smeni-korisnik/).

## How long a sign-in lasts
The administrator decides that for the whole company:
1. At the top right, click your name, then **Settings**.
2. In **Company**, down in the **Session** section, enter in **Login stays valid (hours)** how many hours. Empty means 7 days.
3. Click **Save**.

A shorter time applies right away, to sign-ins already open as well.

> **Important**
> The app allows longer than 7 days, but it warns you: if someone steals the sign-in, it is valid until the end of that time. On a computer that several people share, keep it shorter.
