If the same customer or supplier was entered twice, the two are merged into one. All documents and payments from one move over to the other.

1. At the top right, click your name, then **Settings**.
2. Click **Duplicates**. Here are the partners with the same tax number or the same name.
3. Choose which partner stays: click the circle in front of its name. None is chosen in advance. Until you choose, below the names it shows how they differ. If you're not sure which one is the right one, click the number next to the name (for example #19) — the partner opens in a new tab.
4. The table shows only the fields where the two differ. For each field, click the value that should stay.
5. Click **Merge** and confirm.
After the merge:
- All documents, payments and other records of the other partner move to the one that stays.
- The roles (customer, supplier), phones, emails and websites of both are combined. None of them is lost.
- The other partner is deactivated. It is not deleted.

> **Important**
> A merge can't be undone. Before **Merge**, check that both really are the same partner.

## If "Duplicates" is missing
The tab is visible only to an account with the **Manage** permission (group **Partners & products**). If you don't see it, ask your administrator.

Inactive partners are not in the list. If it says "No duplicates", there are no two active partners with the same tax number or the same name.
