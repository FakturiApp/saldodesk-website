Once you file the return with UJP (the Public Revenue Office), mark it in the app too. That way the books for that period stay exactly as you reported them.

1. At the top, click **Reports**, then **VAT**, then **VAT-04**.
2. Use the arrows ‹ and › to go to the period you filed.
3. If you changed any white fields, click **Save** first.
4. Click **Mark as filed**.

5. Read the message and click **Mark as filed** in the window.

Below the period it now says that it is filed and locked. The form stays as it was filed, and that is how it prints. Its fields no longer change.

> **Important**
> From then on, everything up to the end of that period, and all periods before it, is locked. No document in the books dated up to that day can be issued, edited, cancelled or deleted. Corrections are made with a storno, a credit note or a received credit note dated today. See [Why can't I change a document from a filed VAT period?](../podnesen-ddv-period/)

## When the button is missing
- The period is still running. It is filed after it ends — that is written above the form.
- This isn't the company's VAT period. If the company files quarterly, you file a quarter, not a month. The setting is in **Settings** → **Company**, the **VAT period** field.
- The period is already filed, or it is before the last filed period.
- Your account doesn't have the **File VAT returns** permission. Ask your administrator.

## Marked by mistake — take the filing back
Only the last filing can be taken back.

1. Open **VAT-04** on the last filed period.
2. Click **Take the filing back**.
3. Write a reason. It can't be done without one.
4. Click **Take the filing back** in the window.

The lock moves back to the end of the previous filed period. Fix what you need, then click **Mark as filed** again. The reason stays below the form, in **Filing history**.

If the books later differ from the filed form, a red notice appears above the form with the fields that changed.
