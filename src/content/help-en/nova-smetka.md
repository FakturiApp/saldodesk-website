Everyone who works in the app needs their own account. Their name appears as "Prepared by" on every document they make.

Only an account with the **Manage accounts** right opens accounts, for example the owner.

1. At the top right, click your name, then **Settings**.
2. Click **Accounts**.
3. At the bottom, in **New account**, enter a **Username**. The person will sign in with it. No spaces: letters, numbers, dot, hyphen, underscore or @, 2 to 50 characters.
4. In **Full name**, enter the real name. It is printed on documents.
5. In **Role (template)**, pick what the person may do. Under the field it says what the role gives:

- "Owner" — everything;
- "Accountant" — documents, e-Invoice, reports, costs, payments, archiving;
- "Cashier / Salesperson" — sales documents and stock, without costs;
- "Employee" — view only;
- "Technician" — offered only when the company has the Service module;
- "Rental operator" — offered only when the company has the Rentals module.

6. If the person needs a little more or less than the role, click **Show every permission** and tick or untick the rights.

7. If you like, open **More details** (under the fields). There you find the email, the language, the default warehouse, the color and picture, and the PIN for quick switching. All of it can be filled in later too.
8. In **More details**, under **First sign-in**, **Must change the password** is turned on. Leave it on: that way the person sets their own password at the first sign-in. Turn it off only if the person will work with the password you get now.
9. Click **Add account**.
10. The temporary password appears at the top. Copy it down and give it to the person.

## The first sign-in
The person signs in with the username and the temporary password. If **Must change the password** is on, the app asks for a new one right away:
1. In **Current password**, they enter the temporary one.
2. In **New password** and **Repeat password**, they enter their own, at least 8 characters.
3. They click **Change and continue**.

That way only that person knows their password. Later the person changes the password themselves in **My profile** — see [How do I change my password, PIN, theme or language?](../moj-profil/).

## When something is gray or does not save
- A right you do not have yourself is gray: nobody can give a right they do not have.
- Only an account that itself has everything can give the "Owner" role.
- If **Show every permission** is not there, your account does not have the **Manage permissions** right. Then you pick only a role.

> **Important**
> The temporary password is shown only once. If you lose it, click **Reset password** in the account's row and you will get a new one.

Later you change the rights with **Edit** — see [How do I change what an employee may do, or turn them off?](../prava-na-smetka/).
