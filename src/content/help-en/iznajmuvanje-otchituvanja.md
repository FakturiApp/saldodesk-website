How many copies the customer pays for is calculated from the machine's meters. So every month, enter what the meters show, ideally around the billing day.

## All machines at once
1. In **Rentals**, click **Readings**. The machines are sorted by customer.
2. In **Reading date**, check the date. It applies to all rows.
3. For each machine, enter the **B/W** meter, and if it has them, **Color** and **Scan** too. Under the field you see the previous reading, and while you type — the difference.
4. If the technician printed test pages, enter them under **Service copies**. They are subtracted; the customer does not pay for them.
5. If you want, pick the toner level.
6. Leave empty the machines you have no new number for.
7. Click **Save readings**.

The top shows how many readings were **Saved** and how many were **Rejected**. A rejected reading stays written in, and the machine's row says why.

## One reading
- In **Readings**, at the bottom, open **Single reading**, pick the machine and enter the meters.
- Or, on the machine's page, click **Readings**, then **New reading**.

## When the month is calculated
The month is calculated when there is a reading close to the billing day in the next month — at most half a month before or after it. For example, if you bill on the 1st, September is calculated with the reading from around 1 October. Without such a reading the month waits — see [How do I invoice the rentals for the month?](../iznajmuvanje-fakturiranje/).

> **Important**
> The machine's meter only goes up. The app does not accept a number lower than the previous reading, or higher than the next one, and it says which reading it clashes with, for example: "The B/W counter 14000 is lower than the previous reading (14946 from 01.10.2026)." Check the number on the machine and enter it again.
