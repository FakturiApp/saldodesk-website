An invoice without goods — electricity, water, phone, rent, accountant, transport — is entered as a **Cost invoice**. It does not change stock. The supplier becomes your creditor, and the invoice goes into the purchase VAT book.

1. At the top, click **Documents**, then **Purchasing**.
2. Click **Cost invoice**, then the blue "+" button. **New cost invoice** opens.
3. Choose the **Supplier** and enter the **Supplier invoice number**.
4. **Invoice date** is empty: enter the date written on the invoice. **Receipt date** is today — the day the invoice reached you.
5. On the line, in the **Cost type** field, choose a type, for example "Electricity". If it is not there, type it and click **Create new**.
6. In **Description**, write what it is, if you like.
7. In **Amount excl. VAT**, enter the amount, and in **VAT** choose the rate: 18 %, 10 %, 5 % or 0 %.
8. Once you enter the amount, an empty row appears under the line. Enter the next line there.
9. Check the **Total incl. VAT** and click **Issue**. If you are not finished yet, click **Save draft**.

When you pay, click **Record a payment** on the invoice.

## The list of cost types
Below the lines, click **Edit list**. In the **Cost types** window you can rename a type, add a new one or delete one. A type that has been used on a line cannot be deleted. The new name applies on all lines with that type.

## A foreign supplier
If the supplier is foreign, **Currency** appears on the screen. When the currency is not MKD, **Exchange rate** appears too — enter it. Such an invoice does not go into the purchase VAT book. You see it in **For the accountant** — see [What does "For the accountant" show (forms 240, 220 and 500)?](../za-smetkovoditelot/).

## If you made a mistake
The invoice has **Edit** and **Void**. On an issued invoice, the supplier, the currency and the exchange rate cannot be changed: void it and enter it again.

Tick **VAT not deductible** only when the VAT on the invoice may not be deducted, for example for entertainment or a passenger car.
