Service has two papers:
- **Intake slip**: when the client leaves the device. It has the client, the machine, the problem, the accessories left, the legal deadline, the fee and deposit, the conditions and both signatures.
- **Completion report**: when the client picks up the device. It has the problem, the diagnosis, the work done, the parts and labor with the amounts, the warranty, and who took the device.

## Printing
1. Open the ticket.
2. At the top, next to the machine's name, click **Intake slip** or **Completion report**.
3. The paper opens. Click **Print**.
4. In the print window, choose a printer, or save it as a PDF.

What prints is what is saved on the ticket. If you changed something, first click **Save** in that part of the ticket.

## How the papers look
1. In **Service**, click the gear button (**Settings**), then **Printed documents**.
2. Choose **Intake slip** or **Completion report**.
3. Tick what should print. You can change the name of each field.
4. In **Texts**, change the **Document title** and **Footer note**. The **Intake slip** also has **Conditions**. With **+ Add your own text** you add a paragraph of your own.
5. On the right is the preview, exactly as it will print. Click **Save**.

**Print a sample** prints a test paper. **Reset to default** brings back the look the app had.

On the intake slip, **Legal deadline** and **Conditions** are required by law. You can rename them, but you cannot remove them. If **Conditions** is empty, the standard text is printed.

If you tick **Ticket QR code**, the intake slip gets a QR code. Scanned in the app, it opens the ticket.
