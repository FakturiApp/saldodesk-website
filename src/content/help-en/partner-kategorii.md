Categories split partners into groups, for example "Wholesale" and "Retail". You can then filter by category in **Partners** and in **Reports**.

## Create categories
1. At the top, click **Partners**.
2. Click **Edit categories**.
3. Click **New category**, write a name and click **Add**. For the next one, click **New category** again.
4. Close the window with ×.

In the same window:
- Click the category's name to rename it.
- ▲ and ▼ change the order.
- **sub-level** makes a subcategory under it.
- 🗑 deletes the category. If a partner has it, it isn't deleted but hidden, and the partners keep it.

## Put a partner in a category
One partner: open it, click the **Other** section, choose the category in **Category** and click **Save**. The **Category** field appears once you have made at least one category.

Many partners at once:
1. In **Partners**, tick the partners (the box left of the name).
2. At the top, click **Put in a category**.
3. Choose a category. To remove the category, choose **No category**.
4. Click **Save**.

**Edit categories** and **Put in a category** are there only for an account with the **Manage** permission (group **Partners & products**). If you don't have them, ask your administrator.

## Filter by category
- **Partners:** click **Columns & filters**, then **+ Add filter**. In **Choose a field**, choose **Category** (you can also type it), then choose the category.
- **Reports:** in the **Partner category** field, choose the category.
