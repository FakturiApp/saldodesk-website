The company's name, address and tax number are printed on every document and go to UJP (the Public Revenue Office) with every e-Invoice. The logo is printed only on the documents; it doesn't go to UJP. Everything is entered once, in **Settings** → **Company**.

1. At the top right, click your name, then **Settings**. **Company** opens.
2. If you know the company's number at UJP, enter it in **EPDD ID (eUJP ID)**. At UJP it is under Profile → User details; it is not the tax number.
3. Enter the **Tax number**. Only the owner can change it.
4. Click **Save**. The connection to UJP is checked with the saved tax number, so save first.
5. Click **Test connection** — it should say "The connection works ✅". If it says "First enter and save the company's tax number.", the tax number isn't saved yet.
6. Click **Fill from UJP**. The name and address fill in by themselves from UJP. The button works when both the tax number and the EPDD ID are entered.

7. Check **Company name**, **Address (street)**, **Number**, **Postal code** and **City**. The address is required for an e-Invoice.
8. Enter **Phone** and **Email**. For another number or address, click **+ add**. The first one is the main one: it is printed first and goes to UJP.
9. Under **Logo**, click **Choose image** and choose the picture from your computer. A big picture is fine — the app shrinks it by itself. If the logo already shows the company's name, tick **The logo contains the name**: the top right of the app then shows only the logo, without the name next to it.
10. Click **Save** (at the top or the bottom).

## Bank accounts
Below the form is the **Bank accounts** section. Each account is added separately and saved by itself, without **Save** from the form.
1. Enter **Bank** and **Account**.
2. For an account in a foreign currency, tick **For a foreign currency** and enter **IBAN**, **SWIFT** and **Currency**.
3. Click **Add account**.

One account is the **Primary** one — it is offered first when you enter a payment. You make another one primary with **Make primary**. With **Remove**, the account is no longer offered; payments that already use it stay the same.

> **Important**
> Without **Save**, nothing from the form is kept. If you leave **Company** before that, what you entered is lost.
