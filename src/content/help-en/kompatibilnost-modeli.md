On every toner, cartridge or part you can write down which machine models it is for. Then for every machine you see right away what fits it, and when a product is out, the app offers a replacement.

## The product's model
1. In **Products**, open the product (for example the toner).
2. Click the **Compatibility** tab.
3. Under **Compatible with**, click in **Add a model…** and type the machine's model.
4. Pick it from the list. If it is not there, click **Create new** under the list.

One product can have as many models as you like. On each model:
- a click on the name opens, in **Products**, all the products for that model, with their stock and prices;
- ✎ renames the model;
- ✕ takes the model off this product only;
- 🗑 deletes the model for the whole company.

## Alternatives
Under the models, in **Alternatives**, are the other products for the same models, with their stock. They are offered as a replacement when this product is out of stock. On each alternative you can click **Pin** (always offer it) or **Block** (never offer it).

## All models
In **Products**, click **Models** (or **All models →** on the tab). Here is every model and how many parts fit it. Click the number of parts to see them. Two entries for the same machine (for example "HP 1102" and "HP LaserJet P1102") — join them with **Merge into…**; the parts move to the one model.

## If the product itself is a machine
If you sell the printer or copier itself, on its **Compatibility** tab click **Mark as a device** and pick which model it is. Then it shows all the parts and supplies for it.

> **Important**
> Before you create a new model, search for it in a few ways (for example "1102"). The same model entered twice under different names splits its parts in two.
