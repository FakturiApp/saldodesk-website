How the invoice and other documents look is set in **Settings** → **Printing**. You set it once, in **Shared look**, and all documents follow it.

1. At the top right, click your name, then **Settings**.
2. Click **Printing**.
3. In **Document look**, leave **Shared look** under **Document**. Pick another document (for example Invoice — Dispatch note) only if it needs to look different from the others.

4. Tick what to print: the logo, the address, the registration number, the phone and email, the customer…
5. For a different name, click the field's name and type a new one.
6. You move the fields below and above the table with the list next to them: beside the customer, above the table or below the table, in which row. One row holds at most three fields.
7. In **Texts**, write a **Document title** or **Footer note** if you want.
8. In **Layout**, pick the font size, margins, company color, and the logo's position and size.
9. On the right, in **Preview**, you see how the page will look.
10. Click **Save**.

Fields with 🔒 are required on the document by law. They cannot be unticked, only renamed.

**Reset to default** brings the shared look back to the starting one; the app asks first.

The columns of the lines table and the total are not set in **Shared look**, but on each document separately. There, the preview shows them as on an invoice.

## One document different
1. Under **Document**, pick the document, for example Invoice — Dispatch note.
2. Above the fields it says "Applies to the whole group …" and it is ticked. Then **Save** also changes all the other documents in the group. If only this document should change, untick it.
3. Change what you need and click **Save**.

The tick comes back every time you pick another document. With **Back to the shared look**, the document follows the **Shared look** again; the texts you wrote on it stay. If you leave without **Save**, the app asks whether to discard the changes.

**Print a sample** prints a test page, marked SAMPLE. It is there only for a single document, not for **Shared look**, and only if the company already has a document of that kind.

## The language of printed documents
At the top, in the **Printing** section, **Language of printed documents** says which language documents are printed in. It applies to the whole company.

## The "Print" button and the number of copies
These two choices are yours alone — each employee sets them for themselves, in **My profile** (at the top right click your name, then **My profile**), in the **Printing** section:
- **The “Print” button**: **Preview first** opens the preview page. **Straight to the print window** opens printing right away.
- **Copies**: how many copies are printed. It can also be changed when you print.
