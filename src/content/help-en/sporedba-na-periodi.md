Two periods side by side are in **Reports** → **Comparison**.

1. At the top, click **Reports**, then **Comparison**.
2. Choose a pair: **This month / last month**, **This month / same month last year**, **Last month / same month a year before** or **This year / last year**.
3. For other dates, click **Custom**. Enter **A from** and **A to** for the first period, **B from** and **B to** for the second, and click **Show**.

4. The table shows **Turnover** (excluding and including VAT), **Profit**, **Margin** and **Documents** — for **Period A**, for **Period B**, and the **Change** in percent. For the margin, the change is in percentage points (p.p.).
5. Below the table, in **Customers** and **Products**, are the ones that **Grew the most** and **Fell the most** between the two periods.
6. If you want only a part, choose a **Customer**, **Product**, **Category**, **Brand** or **Warehouse**.
7. **Export to Excel** takes the same comparison.

The pairs with this month and this year count only up to now. For example, this month up to today is compared with last month up to the same day, not with the whole month.

## Month by month against last year
For each month of the year against the same month last year, go to **By period**, choose **By month** and tick **Compare with last year**. See [How much did we sell and earn in a period?](../izveshtaj-po-period/).

Only an account with the **Costs & margins** permission sees **Profit** and **Margin**.
