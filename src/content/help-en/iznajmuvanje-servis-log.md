Every rented machine has its own service log: a date and a note. Write down visits, delivered toner and anything else worth remembering.

## On the machine
1. In **Rentals**, click **Machines**, then click the machine.
2. Click **Service**.
3. Enter the **Date** and a **Note**.
4. Click **Add**.

You fix an entry with the pencil ✏ (**Edit entry**) and delete it with ✕.

## On the agreement
In the agreement, click **Service logs**. Here are the entries for all the machines on the agreement. For a new entry, pick the **Machine**, enter the **Date** and a **Note**, and click **Add**. You delete an entry with ✕; you fix it on the machine's page.

You can add a new entry only on a machine that has an agreement.

## Who changed what
In the agreement, **History** shows every change: to the agreement, to the terms of each machine and to the service log — who, when and what.
