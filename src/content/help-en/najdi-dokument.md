All documents are in **Documents**.

1. At the top, click **Documents**.
2. Pick the group: **Sales**, **Purchasing**, **Credit notes** or **Internal**. Invoices and dispatch notes are in **Sales**.
3. If you want only one type, click it, for example **Invoice — Dispatch note**. One more click on it brings them all back.
4. Click in the empty field above the table (to the left of **All statuses**) and type a number, part of the partner's name or a product name. The list changes as you type. You can also type in Latin letters.

5. Click the document to open it.

## If it is not in the list
- Cancelled documents are not in the ordinary list. Click **Cancelled**.
- Nor are archived ones. Click **Archived**.
- If **Hide drafts** is on, drafts are not shown. Click it again to bring them back.
- The field next to the search should say **All statuses**.
- Maybe the document is in another group: for example, a goods receipt is in **Purchasing**.

## Searching from anywhere
You can also find a document from the **Search** field at the top, on any page — for example "invoice 7". See [How do I find something in the app?](../prebaruvanje/).
