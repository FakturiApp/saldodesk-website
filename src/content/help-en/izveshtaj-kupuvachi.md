It is all in **Reports** → **Customers**.

1. At the top, click **Reports**, then **Customers**.
2. Pick a period, just as in **By period**: **This month**, **Last month**… or **Custom period**.
3. Pick which customers:
   - **All** — everyone who bought in the period.
   - **New** — those who bought for the first time in this period.
   - **Lost** — those who bought in the previous period but did not buy in this one. The previous period is the same length of time before this one: for **Last month**, the month before it; for **This month**, last month up to the same day; for **Custom period**, the same number of days right before it (for 1.10–31.10 that is 31.08–30.09).
4. Pick the order: **By turnover**, **By documents**, **By profit** or **Lowest margin**.

5. Click a customer's name to open their card.
6. **Export to Excel** takes the same list, with the same filters.

The **Last purchase** column is the day the customer last bought — with an invoice, an export invoice or a fiscal receipt.

> **Important**
> For **Lost**, the numbers are from the previous period, not this one. That is the turnover that left with those customers. Above the totals it says which period that is.

Fiscal receipts without a customer are gathered in one row, "Fiscal receipts — no customer".

Only an account with the **Costs & margins** right sees **Profit** and **Lowest margin**.
