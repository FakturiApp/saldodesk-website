You make a debit note when the customer has to pay more than the invoice says: a price that was too low, forgotten transport, an extra cost. The goods do not move — only the money changes.

## For one invoice
1. Open the invoice and click **New document from this one**.
2. Under **Correction of**, click **Credit/debit note (issued)**.
3. Next to **Type**, click **Debit**. Leave **Mode** at **Correct an invoice**.

4. On the line you are fixing, enter either **Correction (qty)** — how many more pieces, or **Amount (money)** — how much more money, without VAT. Not both on the same line. The price, discount and VAT are taken from the invoice.
5. Write a **Reason**.
6. Click **Issue**.

## Without an invoice
For a bonus, promotion or cost that is not tied to one invoice:

1. At the top, click **Documents**, then **Credit notes**.
2. Click **Credit/debit note (issued)**, then the blue "+" button.
3. Next to **Type**, click **Debit**, and next to **Mode**, click **Standalone (bonus/promo)**.
4. Pick the **Customer**.
5. For each line, enter a **Description**, the **Amount** without VAT and the **VAT**.
6. Write a **Reason** and click **Issue**.

A debit note made from an invoice is sent to UJP (the Public Revenue Office) just like the invoice — see [How do I send the invoice to UJP?](../e-faktura-ujp/).

A wrong debit note that has not been sent to UJP is cancelled with **Cancel**.

If the customer has to pay less, you do the same with **Type** **Credit** — see [Storno or credit note — which one when?](../storno-ili-ko/).

## A rejected or stornoed invoice
A rejected invoice is fixed with a **Storno**, a return note for the goods and a new invoice — see [Storno or credit note — which one when?](../storno-ili-ko/).

On a stornoed invoice, the storno has already returned the whole amount. For returned goods, make a return note — see [The customer is returning goods — how do I make a return note?](../povratnica/). If the storno is a mistake, cancel it first.

> **Important**
> You do not make a debit note on an invoice that the customer rejected at UJP, or that has been stornoed.
