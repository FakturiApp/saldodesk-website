You make an offset when the same partner is both your customer and your supplier: they owe you on your invoices, and you owe them on theirs. Instead of both of you paying, the debts close against each other. No money moves.

1. At the top, click **Documents**, then **Credit notes**.
2. Click **Offset**, then the blue "+" button.

3. In **Partner**, pick the partner. Two columns appear: **They owe us** — your open invoices to them, and **We owe them** — their open invoices to you.
4. Tick the invoices that go into the offset, on both sides.
5. Check at the bottom: **Offset** is the smaller of the two totals. **Remaining** is how much stays open on the bigger side.
6. Click **Save**.

The offset gets a number, and the ticked invoices are closed up to the amount offset. With **Print** you print the offset statement, for both companies to sign.

## Good to know
- **Save** works only when something is ticked on both sides.
- Invoices in a foreign currency do not go in: import invoices, export invoices and a **Cost invoice** from a foreign supplier.
- A wrong offset is cancelled with **Cancel**. The offset stays on record as cancelled, and the invoices on both sides are open again.
