The answer is in **Stock snapshots**. A snapshot is the stock recorded on a given day: how much of each product there was in each warehouse, at what cost price and what it was worth. The app takes it by itself at the end of every month. Later sales and purchases don't change it.

1. At the top, click **Products**.
2. Below the title, click **Snapshots**.
3. At the top, choose the date. Next to the date it says how many rows the snapshot has.

4. The table has every product that had stock: **Warehouse**, **Quantity**, **Unit cost** and **Value**. At the top right is the **Total value** of all the stock that day. **Unit cost**, **Value** and **Total value** are visible only to an account with the **Costs & margins** permission.
5. For the table in Excel, click **Excel**.

If there is no snapshot yet, the screen says "No snapshots yet. The first one is taken at the end of the month." There is no table yet, but **Take a snapshot** is there: you can take the first snapshot right away (see below) or wait for the end of the month.

## A snapshot on another day
Sometimes you need a snapshot on the day of the stocktake, not at the end of the month. You can also take the snapshot later, for a day that has already passed.
1. Open **Snapshots**.
2. In **As of**, choose the day you need — today or a past day.
3. Click **Take a snapshot**, then **Confirm**.

For a past day, the app works out the stock as it was at the end of that day, from the stock history: sales, purchases, transfers and later prices after that day are not included. For today's date, the stock is recorded as it is now.

If there is already a snapshot for today's date, the app asks whether to replace it with the current state. If there is already a snapshot for a past day, the app doesn't replace it — first delete it with **Delete snapshot**, then take it again. A deleted monthly snapshot doesn't come back by itself. **Take a snapshot** and **Delete snapshot** are there only for an account with the **Stocktake** permission.
