## All products at once
1. At the top, click **Products**.
2. Below the heading, click **Stock**.

<figure class="shot"><span class="shot-frame" style="aspect-ratio:1280/720"><img src="/help-img/en/products-zalihi.webp" alt="The Stock tab in Products" width="1280" height="720" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><span class="shot-ring" style="left:8.58%;top:28.10%;width:4.88%;height:4.44%"></span><span class="shot-tag is-above is-right" style="bottom:71.90%;left:11.02%">Click here</span></span><figcaption>The Stock tab in Products</figcaption></figure>
3. For each product you see **In stock**, **Minimum stock**, **Cost price** and **Stock value**. Below the table are the totals: how many products, how many pieces and what they are worth. The **Minimum stock** column (and the **Below minimum** label) is there only if the company has the "Purchasing" module. Only an account with the **Costs & margins** permission sees **Cost price** and **Stock value**.
4. If the company has more than one warehouse, choose one in the **All warehouses** field. Then the figures are for that warehouse only.

**Stock** shows only the products whose stock is not zero (and those below zero). If you want to see the ones without stock too, click **Columns & filters** and untick **Only with stock**.

What the colors and labels mean:
- A red figure with a minus means more was sold than there was. The stock comes back when the goods arrive — see ["Not enough in stock" — what do I do?](../nema-zaliha/).
- **Below minimum** next to the name means the stock is below the product's minimum stock.
- "in transit" means some of the goods are on their way between two warehouses. That part cannot be sold until it is received.

## One product
1. In **Products**, click the product.
2. Click the **Stock** tab.

<figure class="shot"><span class="shot-frame" style="aspect-ratio:1280/900"><img src="/help-img/en/products-zaliha-tab.webp" alt="The product's stock by warehouse" width="1280" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><span class="shot-ring" style="left:7.11%;top:26.14%;width:4.88%;height:3.78%"></span><span class="shot-tag is-above is-right" style="bottom:73.86%;left:9.55%">Click here</span></span><figcaption>The product's stock by warehouse</figcaption></figure>
3. At the top is the **Total on stock** for the whole company. The table shows the **Quantity** in each warehouse.
4. Below the table are **Total purchased** and **Total sold**, with the date of the last purchase and sale.
5. Every document that brought the product in or took it out, with the balance after it, is on the separate **Stock card** tab. Click the tab, then click a document to open it.

In the **Location** column you can write where the product sits in that warehouse (shelf, row, box). It is saved as soon as you click outside the field.

You also see the stock while you are writing an invoice: under the line it says **Stock** — how much there is **here** (in the document's warehouse) and **total**.

Stock is not changed by hand. It changes only through documents: a goods receipt increases it, an invoice reduces it. If the figure is not right, do a stocktake — see [How do I do a stocktake?](../popis/) and [Write-off, adjustment or disposal — when to use which?](../trebovanje-nivelacija-otpis/).
