When several people work on one computer, you do not have to sign out every time. With **Switch user** you switch over in a few seconds.

1. At the top right, click your name.
2. Click **Switch user**.
3. Click the name of the person who will work.
4. If the app asks, enter their password or PIN and click **Switch**.

Everything done from that moment on is under the new person's name.

If there is something unsaved on the screen, the app first asks "You have unsaved changes — continue?". A new document you started is not lost: when you come back to your account and open the same screen, the app offers to bring it back with **Restore**.

## What switching asks for: password, PIN or nothing
This is set for each account separately, by someone who manages the accounts:
1. **Settings** → **Accounts**, then **Edit** in the account's row.
2. In **Switch mode**, choose **Password**, **PIN** or **No confirmation (instant)**.
3. Click **Save** under the field.

- **PIN** can be chosen only if the account already has a PIN. Each person sets their own PIN in **My profile**, in **Quick-switch PIN** — see [How do I change my password, PIN, theme or language?](../moj-profil/).
- An account that may manage accounts or settings cannot switch with **No confirmation (instant)** — for it, choose **Password** or **PIN**.

## Locking when nobody is working
The app can lock itself when nobody is using it:
1. **Settings** → **Company**, the **Session** section.
2. In **Auto-lock (minutes)**, enter how many minutes. Empty means it does not lock.
3. Click **Save**.

The locked screen says "Locked". Click your name and enter what it asks for, then carry on where you left off.
