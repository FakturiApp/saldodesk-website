When the goods have already left with a dispatch note (see [How do I make a dispatch note?](../ispratnica/)), you make the invoice from it. That way the goods don't leave stock a second time.

## From one dispatch note
1. In **Documents**, click **Dispatch note**, then open the dispatch note.
2. Click **Make invoice**. The invoice is issued right away, with the same lines and prices.
If you want to change something first (price, discount, payment terms), click **Invoice with changes** instead. The invoice opens as a draft. Change what you need, click **Save**, then **Issue**.

## From several dispatch notes for the same customer
1. In **Documents**, click **Dispatch note**.
2. Tick the dispatch notes (the box to the left of the number).
3. Click **New document from selected**, then **Invoice — Dispatch note**.

4. **New document** opens with the lines from all the dispatch notes. Check them and click **Issue**.

The dispatch notes on one invoice must have the same delivery date. If they differ, the app says which ones — then make a separate invoice for each date.

The invoice's **Date of supply** is taken from the dispatch note.

> **Important**
> Once you make the invoice, the dispatch note can no longer be changed. If something needs fixing, fix it on the invoice.
