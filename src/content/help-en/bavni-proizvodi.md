These are the slow sellers: they have stock, but they have not sold for a certain number of days. You find them in **Reports** → **Slow sellers**.

1. At the top, click **Reports**, then **Slow sellers**.
2. Pick how many days without a sale: 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365. If you don't pick, it is 90 days.
3. If you want only part of them, pick a **Category**, **Brand** or **Warehouse**.

4. The top shows how many products are slow (**Slow sellers**), how many units are sitting (**Units in stock**) and what they are worth (**Value sitting idle**), with their share of all the stock.
5. Pick an order: **By value**, **Idle longest** or **By quantity**.
6. Click a product to open its card.
7. **Export to Excel** takes the same list.

In the table, **Last sold** is the date the product was last sold. **Days unsold** is how many days have passed since then.

## What "has not sold" means
- A sale is an invoice, an export invoice or a printed fiscal receipt.
- A product that has never sold is counted from the day it arrived. That is why a new product is not slow.

## Thin margins
Products that sell, but earn very little, are in **Thin margins → products by lowest margin**. That opens **Products**, sorted by **Lowest margin**.

The value, the **By value** order and the thin-margins link are seen only by an account with the **Costs & margins** permission.
