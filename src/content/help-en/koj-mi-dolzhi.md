Everything your customers owe you, and how late they are, is in one place. For how much one partner owes — see [How much does the partner owe me?](../partner-dolg/).

1. At the top, click **Reports**.
2. Click **Money**.

## What is on the screen
- **Cash calendar** — how much should come in and go out, week by week, based on the due dates of open invoices. What should already have arrived is in a separate row, **Overdue**.
- **Ageing** — how much your customers owe you, split by how late it is: **Not due** (the due date has not passed yet), **1–30 days**, **31–60 days**, **61–90 days** and **Over 90 days**. The sum is in **Total**.

- **Cash flow by month** and **Per account** — how much money came in and went out.
- **To collect** — only the invoices that are already late, oldest first. For each: **Partner**, **Document**, **Due**, **Remaining** and **Days late**. A click on the row opens the invoice, and a click on the name opens the partner.

In **To collect** you can search by partner or document number. With **Columns & filters** you can add more columns, for example the partner's phone and **Last payment**.

## When the lateness is counted from
From the invoice's payment due date. An invoice without a due date is treated as due on the invoice date — it is late from the next day.

## Penalty interest
If **Calculate penalty interest** is turned on in **Settings** → **Company**, **To collect** also has a **Penalty interest** column. You enter the rates there, in the same section.

If you do not see **Money** in **Reports**, your account does not have the **View payments & debt** right. Ask the administrator.
