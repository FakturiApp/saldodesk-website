You make an invoice in **Documents**. When you issue it, it gets a number, and the goods leave stock.

1. At the top, click **Documents**.
2. Click the blue "+" button at the end of the row of document types.

3. **New document** opens. At the top, check that **Invoice — Dispatch note** is selected.
4. In **Partner**, type part of the customer's name and pick them from the list.
5. The **Date** is today. If the customer has agreed payment terms, **Payment due** fills in by itself.
6. If the company has several warehouses, pick in **Warehouse** which one the goods leave from.
7. In the **Product** field, type a name, code or barcode and pick the product. The **Price** fills in by itself, and if the customer has an agreed discount, **Disc. %** fills in too.
8. Enter the quantity in **Qty**. If needed, change the **Price** or enter a **Disc. %**.
9. For another product, type in the empty row below it (or click **+ add line**).
10. Check the amount at **Total incl. VAT**.
11. Click **Issue**.

The invoice opens, now with its own number. From here you can print it — see [How do I print the invoice?](../pechati-faktura/).

> **Important**
> **Issue** issues the invoice right away: it gets a number, and the goods leave stock. If you are not done yet, click **Save draft**. The difference is explained in [What is a draft, and what is an issued invoice?](../nacrt-ili-izdadena/).

If the "Not enough in stock" window opens after **Issue**, see ["Not enough in stock" — what do I do?](../nema-zaliha/).

If the goods have already left with a dispatch note (see [How do I make a dispatch note?](../ispratnica/)), make the invoice from it — see [How do I make an invoice from a dispatch note?](../faktura-od-ispratnica/).
