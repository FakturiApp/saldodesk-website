Everything that is yours alone — the password, the PIN, the email, the recovery code, the theme, the language, the buttons, the tables, the document colors and how amounts look — is in **My profile**. The changes apply only to your account.

At the top right, click your name, then **My profile**.

## Change your password
1. In **Change password**, in **Current password**, enter the password you are signed in with now.
2. In **New password** and in **Repeat password**, enter the new one, the same both times.
3. Click **Change the password**. It says "Password changed."

If it says "Wrong current password.", the current password is not right. If it says "The passwords do not match", the new one was not the same both times.

> **Important**
> When you change your password, your PIN is deleted, and switching to your account asks for the password again. If you switch with a PIN, set a new PIN (below), then ask the administrator to turn PIN switching back on. Every other place where you were signed in with this account is signed out.

## Set a PIN
The PIN is for switching quickly to your account on a computer that several people share (**Switch user** in the menu under your name).
1. In **Quick-switch PIN**, in **Current password**, enter your password.
2. In **New PIN** and **Repeat PIN**, enter 4 to 6 digits.
3. Click **Set the PIN**.

Under the button it says the **Switch mode**: with a password, with a PIN or right away. You do not change that; the administrator does, in **Settings** → **Accounts**.

## Enter your email
This is optional. With the email you can sign in instead of with the username.
1. In **Email**, in **Your email**, enter the address.
2. In **Current password**, enter your password.
3. Click **Save the email**. It says "Email saved."

If you empty the field and save, the email is removed. If it says "This email is already used in this company.", someone else in the company already has it — either as an email or as a username.

## Make a recovery code
With the recovery code you get back in by yourself if you forget your password — every account can have its own.
1. In **Recovery code**, in **Current password**, enter your password.
2. Click **Make a recovery code**. The app asks whether you are sure, because the old code stops working. Click **Make a new code**.
3. The new code is shown. Click **Print** or copy it down. It cannot be seen again.
4. Tick **I have written the code down somewhere safe.** and click **Done**.

If it says "Wrong current password.", the password is not right. For how to get in with the code — see [I forgot my password — how do I get back in?](../zaboravena-lozinka/).

## Theme and language
In **Appearance and language**:
- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Follow Windows** (however the computer is set).
- **Language**: Macedonian, English or Shqip.

You can also change the theme quickly with the moon or sun button at the top, next to your name.

The same card also has **Buttons and tabs** and **Text size in tables**. Below it are **Opening tab** and, at the bottom of the page, **Document colours** — see [How do I change the buttons, tables and colors on the screen?](../izgled-programa/).

## How amounts look
In **Money format**, under **Number style**, pick **Same as the company** or one of the three styles (1.500,00 · 1 500,00 · 1,500.00). Under **Denar label**, pick den., MKD in Cyrillic letters, or MKD in Latin letters. This applies only to your screens — printed documents always look the way the company chose.

You type the same way in every amount field: with 1.500,00, "1.500" is one thousand five hundred, and "1,5" is one and a half.

## What opens when you click
In **Windows and cards** you choose, just for yourself, how windows and cards behave: whether a click on a name opens a small window or goes to the record, how the credit limit warning is shown, and the like. Here is also the **Help button**: **Bottom right** or **In the top bar**.
