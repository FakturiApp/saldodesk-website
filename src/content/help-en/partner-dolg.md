Open the partner: in **Partners**, click their name. Below the name it says **Debt** — how much they still owe us. For a supplier it says **We owe them**.

There are two tabs for the details:
- **Open items** — only the documents that are not paid yet.
- **Account card** — all documents and all payments in order, with the balance after each row.

## Open items
1. Click **Open items**.
2. The totals are at the top: **Receivable** (they owe us), **Payable** (we owe them), **Difference**, **Advance from them** and **Advance to them**.
3. A partner that is both a customer and a supplier has both sides. Click **They owe us** or **We owe them** for just one side. **All** brings back both.
4. In the table, for each document: **Due** is the payment deadline, **Remaining** is how much still has to be paid, and **Days late** is how many days it is late.

Click a document to see it.

## Account card
1. Click **Account card**.
2. Choose a view. **By date** gives all documents and payments by date, and the **Balance** column shows how much the debt is after each row. **By document** gives each document with its payments below it and how much remains; here you can tick **Open only**.
3. In **From** and **To**, choose a period. The card opens from the start of the year. Whatever came before that is in the **Opening balance** row.
4. **All**, **Customer** or **Supplier** — which side to view.
5. In **By date**, you choose what to view with **Documents** and **Payments**.
6. For paper, click **Print**; for a spreadsheet, **Excel**. Both take the whole card, not just what is on the screen.

A balance above zero means the partner owes us. A balance below zero means we owe them. All amounts are in denars.

## Who owes me — everyone at once
For a list of everyone who owes you, in **Partners** click **Who owes me (Collections)** — see [Who owes me, and how late are they?](../koj-mi-dolzhi/).

## If the tabs are missing
**Open items**, **Account card** and **Debt** are visible only to an account with the **View payments & debt** permission. If you need them, ask your administrator.
