Some forms for UJP (the Public Revenue Office) are made only by the accountant. The app does not make them, but it shows the events in the period that may call for one of them. The accountant decides.

1. At the top, click **Reports**, then **VAT**.
2. Click **For the accountant**.
3. Use the arrows ‹ and › to go to the period.

4. At the top it says how many events there are for each form.
5. In the table, the **Deadline** is the 15th of the following month.
6. Click a row to open the document.
7. **Export to Excel** takes the list for the whole period.

## Which event, which form
- **Possible internal invoice 240** — a cost invoice from a foreign supplier (a service from abroad). It is not in the purchase book. The accountant enters it in VAT-04, in fields 12–15.
- **Possible internal calculation 220** — a write-off, a stocktake shortage, and an adjustment that reduced stock. **Purchase value** is what the goods that left stock were worth.
- **Report on a completed import 500** — every foreign-currency invoice.

Only an account with the **Costs & margins** permission sees **Purchase value**. The whole screen needs **View reports** and **View payments & debt**.
