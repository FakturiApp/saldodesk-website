The **Order list** is a working list: what needs to be ordered and from whom. It is not a document — it is not sent to UJP (the Public Revenue Office) and it does not change the stock.

The list is there only when the company has the "Purchasing" module, and only an account with the **Order list** right sees it.

## How a product gets on the list
- From the **Low stock** screen, with the **Add to order** button.
- From the "Not enough in stock" window, with **Add to order**.
- While you write a document: when you pick a product, the bar under the line has **Add to order**.

The quantity is the product's **Reorder quantity**, but never less than what is missing. If the same product is already on the list for the same supplier, no second row is added.

## How to order
1. At the top, click **Products**, then **Order list**.
2. The products are split by supplier. Check the **Quantity** on each row and change it if needed.
3. Click **Print** next to the supplier to print an order for them. It has only the rows that have not been ordered yet.
4. When you have ordered, click **Ordered** on the row, or **All ordered** for all the rows of that supplier.
5. Remove a row you do not need with **Remove**.

A row leaves the list by itself when you issue a goods receipt or a receipt invoice with that product.

## No supplier
A product without a default supplier, added while a document was being written, waits in the **No supplier yet** section. (A product that has a default supplier goes straight to them.) For each one, pick a **Supplier** and click **Assign**. The supplier is also saved on the product, for next time.

The list is only a reminder. The order is not sent to the supplier by itself — print it or send it yourself.
