In **My profile**, each employee sets up the screen for themselves — even an employee with no permission at all for **Settings**. The changes apply only to your account and show right away — there is no **Save**.

At the top right, click your name, then **My profile**.

## What you can change
In **Appearance and language**:
- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Follow Windows**.
- **Buttons and tabs**: how much the buttons stand out. If you find them hard to notice in the light theme, pick **Strong**. The default is **Medium**.
- **Text size in tables**: **Smaller** or **Smallest** shows more rows on one screen. It applies only to the lists.

In **Opening tab**, pick which tab opens when you open a **Product** or **Partner**, for example **Stock** instead of **Basic**.

**Document colours**, at the bottom of the page, says what each color in the document list means. In **Change a colour** you can change the colors for yourself. In **Outline thickness**, pick **Thin**, **Medium** or **Thick**.

What opens when you click a name in a table (a small window or the record) is chosen in **Windows and cards**, and how amounts are written on your screen (1.500,00 · 1 500,00 · 1,500.00) in **Money format** — both also in **My profile**. See [How do I change my password, PIN, theme or language?](../moj-profil/).

Only an account allowed at least one company setting sees **Settings** in the menu under your name.
