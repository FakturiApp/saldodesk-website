Any reading can be corrected or deleted from the machine's page.

1. In **Rentals**, click **Machines**, then click the machine.
2. Click **Readings**.
3. Next to the wrong reading, click the pencil ✏ (**Correct reading**).

4. Correct the meters, **Service copies**, the toner or the note.
5. Click **Save**.

A corrected reading must also be between the previous and the next one, otherwise the app does not accept it.

If you correct the machine's first reading, the starting meter counters in the contract are corrected with it, so the two are always the same.

## Wrong date
The reading's date cannot be corrected. Delete the reading with the bin 🗑 (**Delete reading**) and enter it again with the right date.

## If the month has already been calculated
The app warns you when the reading is in a month that has already been calculated:

- If the invoice is still a draft: after the correction, delete the calculation in **Billing** and calculate the month again — see [The rental month was calculated wrong — how do I calculate it again?](../iznajmuvanje-presmetka-odnovo/). A draft for several machines is deleted as a whole, for all the machines on it — ✕ asks first.
- If the invoice has been issued: the correction is saved, but the invoice does not change. Correct it with a credit note or a debit note in **Documents** — see [Storno or credit note — when to use which?](../storno-ili-ko/).

> **Important**
> Don't cancel an issued rental invoice: the month stays calculated and is not calculated again. If it has already been cancelled, open it in **Documents**, with the **Cancelled** button. If it has **Edit and issue again**, click it: the invoice is corrected and issued again, under the same number.
