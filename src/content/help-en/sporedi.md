**Compare** puts two to five things of the same kind side by side — for example two partners, three products or two quotes — and shows how they differ. It changes nothing.

## From the list
1. In the list (for example **Partners**), tick two to five things — the box to the left of the name.
2. A bar with **Quick comparison** and **Compare** appears above the table.
3. **Quick comparison** opens a small window with only **Overview** and **Key differences** — the rows where the items differ most. The other sections are in the full comparison: **Open the full comparison** at the top of the window.
4. **Compare** opens the full comparison right away.

**Clear selection** removes the ticks.

You can also start from the card: on a partner, product or document, click **Compare** next to the name. The comparison opens with it, and you add the others by searching.

## The Compare screen
- The items are at the top. × removes an item. In **Add another** (**Search and add**) you add a new one. When comparing warehouses, there is no search: each warehouse is its own button — click the warehouse you are adding.
- **Key differences** are the rows where the items differ most.
- **Differences**, **Similar** and **All** choose which rows to see: only the different ones, only the same ones, or all of them.
- Below them are the sections, for example **Overview** and **Finance**. A closed section opens when you click its name.
- **Period** applies to the sections that count over time, for example turnover.

"—" means there is no data — it is not zero. A value your account may not see is locked, and no difference is shown for it.

## Save, print, Excel
- **Save** keeps the comparison: write a name and click **Save**. When you open it later, the figures are worked out again.
- **Print** prints the comparison, with only the open sections.
- **Download Excel** takes the same comparison into Excel.

Saved comparisons are in **Saved comparisons**: at the top right, click your name, then **Compare**. There are the comparisons you have saved. Clicking the name opens one; each also has **Rename** and **Delete**. From the same place you can also start an empty comparison: choose what you are comparing (**What are you comparing**), then add the items.
