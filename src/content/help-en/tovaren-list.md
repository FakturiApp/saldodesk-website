A waybill for domestic transport of goods is made either empty, for the technicians' trips, or from a dispatch note or invoice.

## An empty waybill
1. At the top, click **Documents**, then **Transport**.
2. Click **+ New waybill**.
3. The sender and the carrier are the company, the recipient is "Various customers", and field 9 has the text from **Settings** → **Transport**.
4. In **4. Registration number**, choose or type the vehicle.
5. Click **Print**.

## From a dispatch note or invoice
1. Open an issued dispatch note or invoice.
2. Click **Waybill**.
3. The customer is in field 2, and the goods in field 7: name, quantity and unit, and gross weight from the product's weight. Marks, number of packages, type of packaging and volume you write in yourself.
4. Every field can be changed. **+ Row** adds a row, **Remove row** takes it out.
5. Click **Print**.

There is no waybill for an export invoice, a draft, or a voided document.

## Saved waybills
If **Save waybills** is turned on in **Settings** → **Transport**, click **Save** first instead of printing. The waybill gets a number by day, for example "0511/26", and sits under **Documents** → **Transport** → **Waybills**. There you print it again and change it with **Save changes**. A waybill you do not need is deleted with **Delete**: it stays in **Deleted** for 30 days and can be brought back from there with **Restore**, then it is deleted for good.
