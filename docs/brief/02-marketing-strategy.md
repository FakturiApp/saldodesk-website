# SaldoDesk — Marketing Strategy (Website project, Stage 3 draft)

*Draft 2026-11-16 for discussion. It builds on `01-application-analysis.md` and the decisions so far:
**hosted in the browser**, and **е-Фактура stays the headline**, with the signing client (ЕФ8) built
separately before launch. Pricing is left for Stage 5.*

The site's copy is Macedonian first. Headlines below are written in Macedonian, with an English
gloss for the team. Albanian versions are translated from the approved Macedonian, never written
separately.

---

## 1. The central question

> *Why should a Macedonian business choose SaldoDesk and pay for it, instead of staying with what it
> has?*

**Our answer, in one sentence:**
the е-Фактура deadline forces every VAT payer to look at their program now. SaldoDesk is the one that
was **built for Macedonian law from the first line**, shows **real profit and real debts**, and works
**in Macedonian and Albanian from any computer**.

---

## 2. Positioning

**Who it is for.** Macedonian small and medium businesses that issue invoices every day and keep
goods in stock: trade, distribution and import, plus service shops and office-equipment companies
through the add-ons.

**What they use today.** Mostly older desktop programs (Акцент is the one the repo knows), Excel
beside them, and an external accountant who re-types things.

**Positioning statement (internal):**
> For Macedonian businesses that issue invoices daily and keep stock, **SaldoDesk is the business
> program in the browser that does documents, stock, money and ДДВ the way Macedonian law requires —
> and is built for е-Фактура.** Unlike older desktop programs, it shows the true profit on every sale
> and who owes what, in Macedonian and Albanian, from any computer, and moves your data over from
> Акцент.

**What we are NOT positioning as:**
- not "all-in-one ERP" or "the future of business" (generic, and the buyer does not believe it);
- not "cheapest";
- not international or English-first.

The depth of Macedonian specifics is the moat. We should show it, not just say it.

## 6. е-Фактура claims: what we may say, and when

This is the riskiest message on the site, so the wording is staged:

| Stage | Condition | Allowed wording |
|---|---|---|
| **Now (pre-launch, content drafting)** | ЕФ8 not built, never sent to УЈП | „**Изградено за е-Фактура**" (built for e-Invoice); describe the flow; no "tested", no "certified" |
| **Launch** | ЕФ8 works **and** at least one invoice accepted by УЈП's test system | „**Подготвено за е-Фактура**" (ready for e-Invoice) |
| **Later** | Live invoices accepted in production at a real customer | „**Испраќа е-Фактури во УЈП секој ден**" (sends e-Invoices daily) + a customer quote |

**Dates:** quote the timeline only as „според Предлог-законот" (according to the draft law), with a
date stamp, on one place on the site (the е-Фактура page), so it is easy to update. Never in the hero.

## 8. Trust-building strategy

The product is new with no public customers, so trust has to come from **transparency and
access**, not from logos and big numbers.

1. **Try it without signing up: a live demo company.** Open "Демо Фирма ДООЕЛ" in the browser, prefilled
   by the existing seeder (`scripts/seed_demo.py`) and reset every night. For a business program,
   seeing your own kind of invoice in 10 seconds beats any video. *(Needs a small build: a
   read-only or reset-nightly demo tenant.)*
2. **Founding customers ("Основачки клиенти").** The first N companies get a fixed price for a period, a
   free migration from Акцент done with them, and a direct line to the people who build it. This turns
   "unproven" into "early and personally supported".
3. **Built in a real business.** The product was developed in and for a company that issues invoices,
   imports, repairs and rents machines every day. Only with the owner's permission to say so, and
   whether Дигитрон may be named.
4. **A public "what's ready / what's coming" page.** An honest list: е-Фактура sending ✓, receiving
   invoices — coming, fiscal — coming, bank import — coming. Accounting buyers reward honesty, and
   the page prevents refunds and angry support calls.
5. **Public help centre.** The 132 Macedonian help articles, with screenshots on fictional data, published
   on the site. It shows depth, ranks in Google for "како да издадам сторно" -type searches, and answers
   objections before they are asked.
6. **Security and data page.** Where the data is hosted, backups, who can see it, export of your data at
   any time. **Only after the hosting is decided** — I will not write claims about it before then.
7. **Real people.** A name, a phone/Viber number and support hours. In the Macedonian SMB market a
   reachable human is the strongest trust signal there is.

**What we deliberately don't do:** no fake testimonials, no "1000+ companies", no stock photos of
handshakes, no invented uptime figures.

## 10. Calls to action

| Role | Wording (mk) | Gloss | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **„Започни бесплатно"** | Start free | Hero, after each pillar, pricing, final section |
| Secondary | „Види демо фирма" | See a demo company | Hero, features |
| Human | „Закажи 15 мин. презентација" | Book a 15-min walkthrough | Pricing, final section, founding-customers |
| Returning | „Најави се" | Log in | Header, top right |

**Decision 2026-11-16 (owner): the core package is paid after a free trial.** The primary CTA
therefore names the trial: **„Пробај бесплатно 30 дена"** (try free for 30 days), with
„Без картичка" (no card) under it. This assumes the trial is 30 days, like the add-ons; still to be
confirmed.

What this needs in the app (none of it exists today; build in a later session):
- **A trial clock for the whole company.** Today only add-ons have trials. The core is always on, and
  the only way to stop a company is the provider's „Блокирај", which locks everyone out completely.
- **What happens at the end of the trial.** Recommended: **read-only with export.** The customer can
  still open, print and export their own documents, but cannot issue new ones until they pay.
  - Locking a business out of its own invoices would be the strongest objection the site could
    create.
  - It would also contradict the app's own rule that "nothing can lock a company out of its own
    invoices".
- **A warning before the trial ends**, which was already an open item in `modules-trials.md`.
- **Recommended simplification:** **one trial, everything included.** During the 30 days every add-on
  is on, then the customer picks what to keep. "30 дена бесплатно, со сите модули" is one simple
  promise. A core trial plus ten separate add-on trials is ten small decisions.

## 11. Brand voice

- **Calm, precise, plain Macedonian.** The voice of a good accountant who explains things well, not a
  startup. Short sentences, concrete numbers, the user's own words (фактура, залиха, долг, ДДВ).
- **Formal „вие"**, as in the app.
- **Show, don't claim.** "Добивката се поправа сама" plus a screenshot beats "моќна аналитика".
- **No hype words:** иновативно, револуционерно, моќно, сè-во-едно, иднината.
- **Honest about limits.** "Доаѓа" is said plainly; it is part of the brand.
- **Albanian is a first-class language.** It is translated and checked by a native speaker, never
  machine-only.
